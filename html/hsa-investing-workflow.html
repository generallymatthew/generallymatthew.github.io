Fidelity

HSA Investing

A self-service investing flow, built to get more HSA holders off the phone and investing their surplus on their own.

Overview

Fidelity contracted me as the UX designer for their HSA product. At the time, investing your HSA surplus meant calling a phone number; there was no self-service path at all. Only 23% of members who held cash beyond their HSA's default ever invested it.

The product team set a target: 45%. My read was that a phone line was never going to get us there. The pitch was a self-service flow built into the main HSA page and the mobile app, direct enough that members could set it up themselves.

I owned that flow for 12 months, working with user research to answer the open questions, then designing every screen in Fidelity's own design system. What's below is a reconstruction, not the real UI: an NDA covers the actual screens, so these wireframes rebuild the logic of the flow without showing Fidelity's design system.
